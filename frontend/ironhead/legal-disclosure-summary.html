Disclosure summary

The full set of IronHead™ customer-facing legal disclosures, indexed by audience so you can find the right document whether you came here from an auction page, a guide landing page, or a checkout receipt.

Effective 2026-08-26One-page hub · all eight disclosures are linked below.1

By audience

  • Buying on the auction floor. Read auction rules first, then buyer disclosures (as-is / mileage caveat / title-risk).
  • Selling on the auction floor. Read seller agreement first (representations you affirm at submission), then auction rules (the buyer-side agreement your listing will be governed by).
  • Buying a guide. Read guides disclaimer (safety, informational use, no-result guarantee). Refunds policy covers guide-specific refund mechanics.
  • Something went wrong. Start with dispute resolution, then check the refunds & cancellations page if the issue concerns a refund or chargeback.
  • Everybody. Terms of service and Privacy cover the standing rules of the site that apply on every page.
