Refunds & cancellations

Plain and short: what you bought, how it arrives, and exactly what we do when something goes wrong. Auction lots are covered at the end, alongside the dispute resolution page.

Effective October 10, 2026.

What you get when you buy a guide

Every IronHead guide is a digital PDF. The moment Stripe confirms your payment, we email you a private download link — it also appears on the order confirmation page. Nothing is shipped and there is nothing to wait on: the guide is yours to download, save, and print for your binder.

If the download never lands

Links get eaten by spam filters and inboxes fill up. If your email does not arrive, the link will not open, or the file comes down corrupted, incomplete, or as the wrong guide, write to bill@aitoolsfortoday.com from the email you used at checkout and tell us which guide you bought. We will re-send the link or replace the file. If we cannot fix it, we refund the purchase in full — a guide you cannot read is not a guide you should pay for.

Duplicates and wrong listings

Bought the same guide twice by accident? Tell us and we refund the duplicate. And if the guide page itself said something the file does not deliver — wrong years, wrong model, wrong contents — that is our error: replacement or a full refund, your call.

Every other request

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. Write to bill@aitoolsfortoday.com from the email you used at checkout, with the guide name and the reason, and we will answer. One honest limit: a downloaded guide cannot be un-downloaded, so we cannot refund a guide that arrived intact and readable simply because it was not the one you wanted. Check the year, make, and model on the guide page before you buy; every title names the exact bike it covers. If we described it wrong, that one is on us — see above.

Chargebacks

A chargeback is a reversal of a card charge run by your bank instead of through us. Where a purchase was actually charged and you dispute it with your bank, the bank runs that process and decides it; we send the bank the order and delivery record. Writing to us first is almost always faster — most problems are a lost email, and we can fix those the same day.

Auctions: cancellations

IronHead auction lots are not bought through checkout here: no buyer sale funds are collected, held in escrow, or transferred through this site. Settlement and delivery are coordinated manually after the hammer. The short version of the cancellation rules:

  • Before the hammer. A seller may edit a live listing at any time, and may cancel a listing before any qualifying bid is placed. After the first qualifying bid, the seller contacts support or opens a dispute before cancelling. If a seller discovers a material misrepresentation, finds the lot unexpectedly unavailable, or no longer has authority to sell it, the platform documents the cancellation and coordinates next steps with both sides.
  • Buyer default. If a winning buyer does not complete the manual settlement and delivery coordination inside the stated window, the lot is re-offered to the runner-up bidder if there is one, and otherwise returns to the seller. A buyer who defaults twice in a rolling twelve months may have bidding privileges limited or revoked.
  • Seller default. If a seller refuses delivery, fails to ship inside the agreed window, or cannot produce the lot in the represented condition at handover, the buyer opens a dispute and we coordinate next steps.
  • Platform cancellation. We may cancel a listing or reverse a transaction for fraud, material misrepresentation discovered after listing, a lot that violates the law, an account suspension, or a security incident. In each case we document the outcome, coordinate next steps with the parties, and consider any listing fee case by case.
  • Listing fees. A seller's listing fee is non-refundable except when the cancellation stems from the platform's own moderation action.

For auction purchases, the refund path for anything actually paid runs through the dispute resolution page. Disputes opened within fourteen days of delivery get the full three-step process (ticket, mediation, then optional arbitration); disputes opened later get ticket and mediation.

How refunds arrive

Refunds go back through Stripe to the same card or payment method you used at checkout, and Stripe emails you a receipt when it processes. How long it takes to show on your statement is up to your bank. IronHead shirts and shop gear are sold through pushrodshop.com; the checkout receipt for those orders names the seller and the policy that applies there.
