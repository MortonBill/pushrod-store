Terms of use

The short version: browsing is free, nothing is sold today, drills are training information (not medical advice), and your beta reviews stay honest because that's the deal.

Last updated: October 7, 2026. Applies to sportrootsdrills.com.

Browsing is free

The drill library is free to browse and search, and the coach waitlist is free to join. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for a card to look around.

Nothing is sold today

Paid enrollment is not open. The prices on the pricing page are planned founding prices — they describe what plans will cost when enrollment opens, not an offer to sell anything right now. No charge can be made on this site today.

The beta program

Beta families receive 5 free drills in exchange for an honest review. Honest means honest: praise, complaints, and "my kid lost interest" all count, and a critical review never costs you the drills. Reviews may be quoted on this site with the first name and context you provide (for example, a sport and age band) — tell us in your review if you'd rather stay anonymous.

Coach waitlist

Coach booking is not open yet. Joining the coach waitlist puts you on the email list for the day verified founding coaches open in your sport; it is not a confirmed session and no payment is taken. When coach profiles appear here, they will describe real, verified coaches. SportRoots can't guarantee any particular training outcome.

Training information, not medical advice

Drills are general training information for healthy young athletes. They are not medical, physical-therapy, or safety advice for any individual child. Train with adult supervision, use equipment appropriate to the age band, and stop if anything hurts. If your athlete is returning from an injury, check with their doctor or trainer before starting.

Site content

The drills and pages on this site belong to SportRoots. You're welcome to print and use drills for your own family or team practice; please don't republish or resell them. Pages may link to our family's other brands and to external sites we don't control.

Changes and questions

When these terms change, the date at the top changes with them. Questions about anything here: use the contact page or email bill@aitoolsfortoday.com.
