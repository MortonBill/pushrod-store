Subscription & refund policy

SportRoots Plus Monthly is $4.99/month and Annual is $39/year; both include a 30-day free trial with no card required and auto-renew until you cancel from your account page. Lifetime is a $99 one-time founding payment and Club is $99/year for up to 5 coaches; both are managed under their stated payment cadence. Private coaching sessions are paid up-front; PENDING_PAYMENT bookings can be cancelled without charge, while confirmed-session refunds are adjudicated by SportRoots support.

What parents ask before signing up

Which age tier should my athlete start in?

U10 covers roughly ages 6–9 and starts at fundamentals and movement skills, U12 (ages 10–13) layers in sport-specific sequencing, U14 (ages 13–14) adds tactical reads and showcase prep, and U18 (ages 15–18) continues through elite-track conditioning. Your athlete can move up once they finish a tier.

Can a whole team, coach, or program buy access?

Yes — coaches and program directors can choose Club for $99/year and up to 5 coaches on one shared plan. Club is for the coaching team allowance described here; it does not create athlete roster profiles or repurpose the legacy bulk-order workflow.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel from your account in two clicks, no phone calls and no retention emails. Plus Monthly and Plus Annual start with a 30-day free trial — no card is collected at sign-up — and renew automatically after the trial unless you cancel in your account.
