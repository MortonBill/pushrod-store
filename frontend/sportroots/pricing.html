What parents ask before signing up

Which age band should my athlete start in?

Start with the drill library band that matches your athlete’s age: U6–U8 (ages 6–8) starts at fundamentals and movement skills, U10–U12 (ages 10–12) layers in sport-specific sequencing, U14–U16 (ages 14–16) adds tactical reads and showcase prep, and U18 (ages 17–18) continues through elite-track conditioning. Your athlete can move up once they finish a band.

Can a whole team, coach, or program buy access?

Not yet — paid enrollment is opening soon. When it opens, coaches and program directors will be able to choose Club at a planned $99/year for up to 5 coaches on one shared plan. Club is for the coaching team allowance described here; it does not create athlete roster profiles or repurpose the legacy bulk-order workflow.

Can I cancel anytime?

Once paid plans open, yes — you will be able to cancel from your account in two clicks, no phone calls and no retention emails. Plus Monthly and Plus Annual will start with a 30-day free trial — no card collected at sign-up — and will renew automatically after the trial unless cancelled. Nothing can be charged today because enrollment is not open.
