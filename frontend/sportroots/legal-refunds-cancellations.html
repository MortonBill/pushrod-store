Refunds & cancellations

SportRoots plans, in plain language: what each one costs, how the free trial works, how to cancel, and what happens if you ask for a refund.

Last updated: October 10, 2026. Applies to sportrootsdrills.com.

The plans

  • Plus Monthly — $4.99/month
  • Plus Annual — $39/year
  • Lifetime — $99, one time
  • Club — $99/year for up to 5 coaches, charged at signup

Plus Monthly and Plus Annual both begin with a 30-day free trial: no card, no charge — cancel before the trial ends and nothing is billed. After the trial, a Plus subscription renews automatically until you cancel. Private 1-on-1 coaching sessions, when coach bookings open, are paid up-front at booking; joining the coach waitlist is free.

How to cancel

Cancel anytime through the Manage section on the pricing page — it opens your Stripe billing page, and cancelling takes two clicks. No phone calls, no retention emails. You keep full access until the end of the billing cycle you are in, and cancelling stops the next renewal.

Refunds

Because the free trial needs no card, a trial can never charge you — there is nothing to refund. For a paid plan, cancelling stops future charges, and you keep full access until the end of the current billing cycle. Lifetime ($99 one-time) and Club ($99/year for up to 5 coaches) refund requests are handled case-by-case by SportRoots support: write to bill@aitoolsfortoday.com from the email on the subscription and tell us what happened. Any refund we issue goes back through Stripe to the payment method you used, and Stripe emails you a receipt when it processes.

Questions

Anything about a charge, a trial, or a cancellation: bill@aitoolsfortoday.com. The pricing page carries the same terms, and the FAQ answers the common billing questions.
