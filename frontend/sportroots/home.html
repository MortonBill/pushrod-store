How SportRoots works

1. Browse free

Every drill in the library is listed by sport and age band — U6–U8, U10–U12, U14–U16, and U18. No account, no card, no trial clock just to look around.

2. Book free

Find a founding coach for your athlete's sport and send a booking request. Booking never requires a paid plan.

SportRoots Plus will unlock the full library experience — printables and saved shelves — at a planned $4.99/month or $39/year. Paid enrollment is not open yet; browsing and booking stay free until it is.
