Match your trade or the software your office runs. Every product page says exactly what its playbook covers.

What's the difference between a playbook, a forms pack, and a bundle?

A playbook ($37) is the workflow system: copy-and-paste prompts and step-by-step procedures for your trade's intake, bids, scheduling, follow-up, and documentation. A forms pack ($19) is the ready-to-use paperwork — intake, estimates, change orders, and sign-offs. A bundle ($49–$59) pairs the two at a discount: most save $7, and the QuickBooks Online + Jobber Starter Pack saves $15.

What do I get when I buy?

PDFs, delivered at checkout: your delivery email carries signed download links for every file in the order. Playbooks run from concise working guides to the 36-page QuickBooks Online edition — page counts are on the product pages. There is no subscription and nothing else to install.

Do I need an AI account or tech skills?

You need access to an AI chat tool to paste the prompts into — the playbooks are written to be tool-agnostic and in plain English. No tech background is required: each workflow is a numbered sequence with the prompt ready to copy, the inputs to fill in, and what to check in the output.
