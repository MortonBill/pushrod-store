Garage gear for people who work on their own cars.

PushRod™ is a garage-gear shop: hats, tees, sweatshirts, shop signs, mugs, and decals designed for the people who'd rather be turning a wrench than watching somebody else do it.

Four doors, one shop

The catalog hangs on four doors. PUSHROD™ Garage Gear is our own air-cooled shop line. Muscle Car covers the big-cube American iron. RestoMod is the modern-performance side of the same cars. Truck is for the classic-truck crowd. Pick a door on the shop page and the racks filter to that line.

Printed when you order it

PushRod gear isn't sitting in a warehouse. When you check out, your order goes to our print partner, who prints it and ships it to you. That's how one shop can carry hundreds of designs across hats, tees, sweatshirts, signs, mugs, and decals without a back room full of boxes.

Part of a family of brands

PushRod is the merch shop in a small family of stores run by the same operator: RestorationEssentials publishes restoration guides for muscle cars and classic trucks, and IronHead does the same for vintage motorcycles. The gear lives here; the guides live there; the garage is the same.
