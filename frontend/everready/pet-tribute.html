A Keepsake for the Pet You Lost

The Pet Tribute Book is a small, gentle keepsake we are making for anyone who has lost a dog, a cat, or another pet they loved — a cover for their photo, their name and dates, and room for the memories that belong with them. It is free, and it always will be.

What's inside

A place for the things you don't want to forget

Not a form to fill out. A keepsake to sit with — one page at a time, in your own words, whenever you're ready. There is no deadline, and there is no right way to do it.

The cover page

Room for a favorite photo, your pet's name and their dates, under two words: In Loving Memory. Print it and it stands on its own; everything after it is for you.

Their story, in your words

Short, gently guided pages — how they came to you, the ordinary days, the things only they did, the kind of friend they were. A few lines is plenty. The prompts do the remembering with you.

Photos that belong with the memories

Spaces beside the words for the pictures you already have on your phone — with captions, so years from now the grandkids know exactly who that dog was and why everybody smiled.

Made to be shared

There is room for one memory from each person who loved them, children included. Print copies for the family, or keep the one by your chair. Either way, it is yours.

They were never "just a pet." This is a small book that knows it.
