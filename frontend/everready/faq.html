Clear answers about getting your family organized before an emergency or loss.

What does EverReady Family help families do?

EverReady Family offers practical tools that help families gather important information, prepare for emergencies, and make next steps easier to find. Start with the free 10-item Family Emergency Checklist.

Does EverReady Family provide legal advice?

No. EverReady Family provides organization tools, not legal advice. Your will and your state's probate law control. When in doubt, talk to an attorney.

Where can I find guidance for family conversations and executor tasks?

EverReady Family publishes guides to conversations worth having with your family, an executor's first 30 days, and paperwork families may face after someone dies.
