PushRod™ Wholesale Partner Agreement (DRAFT)

Version 2026-09-30-draft · AI Tools for Today, LLC ("Seller")

1. Appointment

Seller appoints the applicant as a non-exclusive authorized reseller of PushRod™ products, identified by a unique partner ID (PTNR-000000 format) issued on approval. Nothing here grants exclusivity by territory, channel, or customer.

2. Wholesale pricing

Approved partners buy at 20% off current retail on launch products: tees (Comfort Colors 1717) $20.80, hats (Yupoong 6606) $19.20, pullovers (Gildan 18000) $35.20. The price schedule may change with written notice. No volume tiers, no Net terms.

3. No resale price cap

Partners set their own resale prices — sell for whatever the market will bear. Seller does not cap the maximum resale price.

4. MAP policy

The unilateral Minimum Advertised Price policy (published at /wholesale/map) is incorporated by reference. Minimum advertised prices equal our retail prices. Enforcement ladder: (1) written notice, correct within 2 business days; (2) 30-day shipment suspension; (3) termination and do-not-sell listing. The policy is unilateral and non-negotiable; it is not a signed price agreement.

5. Permitted and prohibited channels

Partners may resell through their own website and/or physical store only. Prohibited: Amazon, eBay, Walmart, and all other third-party marketplaces; masked or undisclosed seller names; knowing sales to parties intending online resale; unapproved sub-distributors.

6. Brand usage

Logos and artwork only as supplied by Seller; no altering marks; no use of PushRod™ in domain names or marketplace store names without written approval; no implying endorsement beyond authorized-reseller status.

7. Orders, minimums, payment, freight

Every wholesale order must contain 25+ units per base product (designs and colors may mix within the same blank). Opening order: 48+ units or $500+ in merchandise. Prepay only — card charged at order time. Partner pays bulk shipping at Seller's cost (shown at checkout). Freight is excluded from minimum calculations.

8. Returns and defects

Final sale except defective or mis-shipped goods. Written defect claims within 7 days of receipt; remedy is replacement or credit at Seller's option; RMA required before any return shipment.

9. Tax compliance

Partner represents that a valid signed resale/exemption certificate is on file for each ship-to state before the first tax-exempt order, and agrees to provide renewals. Absent a valid certificate, Seller collects and remits sales tax. Partner is liable for certificate misuse. Records kept 5 years.

10. Term and termination

Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Seller may terminate immediately for fraud, counterfeiting, marketplace diversion, or MAP-policy violation. On termination: cease all trademark use, pay outstanding invoices, and accept placement on the do-not-sell list.

11. Audit, confidentiality, assignment

Seller may request sell-through reporting and audit channel compliance. Partner keeps Seller's non-public business information confidential. No sub-distribution or assignment without Seller's written consent.

12. Governing law and amendments

Governed by the laws of the State of Iowa. Seller may amend this agreement and its policies with written notice; continued ordering after notice constitutes acceptance.
