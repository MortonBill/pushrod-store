Affiliate Disclosure

When a supply link on a Stitchfolk page carries an affiliate tag, this disclosure explains how commissions work — at no extra cost to you — and how we keep recommendations editorially independent.

Baseline legal information — not legal advice

These documents describe Stitchfolk's current product and site practices as a starting point for legal review. They are not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Stitchfolk sometimes recommends tools, yarn, needles, hooks, and other supplies. When a recommendation link carries an affiliate tag, this disclosure explains what that means. It applies to stitchfolkpatterns.com — our one public site. There is no partners directory or partners page. Where a page carries no affiliate link, this disclosure simply does not come into play until one appears.

1. Programs we may use

Supply links, when they appear, may run through affiliate programs such as:

Amazon Services LLC Associates Program.

Michaels affiliate program.

Knit Picks Creators program.

2. How commission works

When you click a tagged link and complete an eligible purchase, Stitchfolk may receive a commission from the vendor. The price to you does not increase because of the link, and Stitchfolk does not receive your payment-card details from that vendor.

A commission is not guaranteed; tracking, eligibility, returns, and vendor terms control whether one is paid.

Affiliate income helps support pattern writing, testing, photography, editing, and site operations.

Vendor privacy, returns, shipping, and product terms apply to the purchase you make on the vendor site.

3. Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not buy a favorable review or guarantee placement. Stitchfolk chooses supplies based on fit, material, usefulness, availability, and maker experience, and may link to a non-affiliate source when that is the better recommendation.

Recommendations reflect Stitchfolk's editorial judgment at the time published.

Prices, inventory, formulas, and vendor policies can change after publication.

You are free to use a substitute supply and do not need to purchase through a tagged link.

4. Link treatment and disclosure

Affiliate links may be marked with a nearby disclosure and use sponsored/nofollow link treatment in the site markup. A sponsored marker describes the commercial relationship; it does not mean the vendor wrote or approved the recommendation.

Where a supply section carries affiliate links, it links to this full disclosure.

A link opening in a new tab remains subject to the destination's terms and privacy policy.

If a relationship changes, the owner should update this disclosure and affected editorial copy.

5. Questions

For a question about a recommendation or affiliate relationship, use /contact. For legal inquiries or privacy matters, contact bill@aitoolsfortoday.com.
