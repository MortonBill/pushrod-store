How it works

  1. Apply — business details, resale certificate, and card on file. Decision within 2 business days; every application is reviewed by a human.

The fine print, up front

  • MAP: advertised prices can't go below our retail (MAP policy). Your actual selling price is yours.
  • Channels: your own website and/or store. No Amazon, eBay, Walmart, or other marketplaces.
  • Tax: a valid resale certificate per ship-to state before your first tax-exempt order — no cert, no tax exemption.
  • Returns: final sale except defective/mis-shipped; defect claims within 7 days.
